DrivePulse Support

Quick Start

  1. Plug your OBD2 adapter (Bluetooth BLE or WiFi) into the car's diagnostic port (usually under the steering wheel).
  2. Turn the ignition on or start the engine (always with the vehicle stopped).
  3. Open DrivePulse on your phone and go to the Connect screen.
  4. Bluetooth (BLE): tap "Search devices" and select the adapter (if prompted, enter the PIN printed on the adapter).
    WiFi: first join the adapter's WiFi network from your phone settings (e.g. "WiFi_OBDII", "VLINK"), then choose "WiFi" and tap "Connect" (default host/port, e.g. 192.168.0.10:35000).
  5. Once connected, DrivePulse will start receiving data!

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Which adapters are compatible and which do you recommend?
DrivePulse supports Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) and WiFi adapters based on the ELM327 chip.
Recommended (iOS and Android): a BLE adapter with auto-sleep, e.g. Vgate iCar Pro BLE 4.0 or Veepeak OBDCheck BLE+. Why: BLE uses very little power on the phone and these models turn themselves off when the engine is off, so you can leave them plugged in without draining the car battery.
WiFi adapters work too (handy on Android or if you already own one), but usually have a higher standby draw: if you leave one plugged in, unplug it when you won't drive for a few days.
Classic Bluetooth (non-BLE) adapters are not supported.
Automatic trip tracking doesn't start, what should I do?
Make sure you granted the "Always" location permission. DrivePulse detects movement and engine RPM in the background; without "Always", iOS will close the app when the phone is locked.
Does the app use a lot of battery?
The app is optimized to minimize consumption. GPS is only activated when the engine is detected as running via Bluetooth. However, on long trips the combined use of Bluetooth and GPS can affect the battery: we recommend keeping the phone charging if possible.
How does the trial work?
You get 7 days of full access to all features from first launch. After 7 days, you'll need a one-time In-App purchase to keep using the app. It's not a subscription: you pay once and DrivePulse is yours forever.
I changed iPhone. How do I restore my license?
Open DrivePulse, go to the purchase screen and tap "Restore Purchases". By signing in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase, the license will be restored automatically at no extra cost.
Can I clear fault codes (DTC)?
For safety, DrivePulse is read-only. It reads and explains fault codes but does not allow clearing them. For clearing codes and repairing the vehicle, always consult a professional.
When does DrivePulse connect automatically to the adapter?
DrivePulse tries to reconnect on its own to the already-paired adapter, with a behavior designed to avoid heating the phone or draining the battery:
  • App open (foreground): if the adapter is plugged in and the ignition is on, the connection starts/resumes within seconds (retries ~every 30 seconds).
  • App in background (phone in pocket/locked, app not closed): it keeps retrying but less often (~every 2–3 minutes) to save power. As soon as you reopen the app, the connection becomes immediate.
  • App closed (swiped away): it won't reconnect by itself — reopen it to reconnect.
For automatic connection and background trip tracking you need Bluetooth on (or the adapter's WiFi network, if you use a WiFi model) and the "Always" location permission. If reconnection with the phone in your pocket is a bit slow, that's intentional: we prioritize battery life; with the app open it's always fast.
