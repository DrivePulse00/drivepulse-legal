DrivePulse Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

In short: DrivePulse is an "Offline by Design" app. We do not collect, transmit or sell any of your data. Everything that happens in DrivePulse stays on your iPhone.

1. No cloud data collection

DrivePulse does not require creating an account. There is no central database or cloud server to which your driving data, GPS location or vehicle information is sent.

2. Locally stored data

All information collected via the OBD2 adapter and the phone sensors (GPS, accelerometer) is stored exclusively in the local database on your device. This data includes:

  • Trip routes and parking locations.
  • Technical engine data (RPM, speed, temperatures).
  • Diagnostic trouble codes (DTC).
  • Vehicle information (VIN, make, model).
  • Vehicle photos (saved in the app's private storage).

3. Use of Location

The app requests "Always" location access exclusively to enable automatic trip tracking in the background. This GPS data is used only within the app to compute distances and display maps. None of your location data is ever shared with third parties or with the developer.

4. Bluetooth communication

Bluetooth is used only to communicate locally with your OBD2 adapter (ELM327). No other connections are established.

5. In-App Purchases

DrivePulse uses Apple's in-app purchase system (StoreKit) for the Lifetime license. Transactions are handled entirely by Apple and the developer neither receives nor stores any payment data. The purchase receipt is managed by your Apple ID.

6. Your rights

Since all data resides on your phone, you have full control: you can export it via the backup feature or delete it permanently by removing the app or using the "Delete all data" function in settings.

7. Safety and responsibility

Never look at or use the phone while driving. Consult DrivePulse only when the vehicle is stopped. The driver is solely responsible for safe driving and for complying with laws on phone use while driving. The values shown are informational estimates and do not replace a mechanic; the app is read-only and does not modify the vehicle. For the full terms and limitation of liability, see the Support page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions you can write to: support@drivepulse.it
